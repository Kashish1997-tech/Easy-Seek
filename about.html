About Us

About EasySeek

EasySeek is a focused educational resource hub built for students of Punjabi University and Thapar University.

Our Story

Why we started EasySeek

Before every exam, students would scatter across WhatsApp groups, random PDFs and outdated blog posts trying to find the right book, the latest result, or last year's paper. EasySeek exists to fix that — one clean, searchable hub for exactly the resources Punjabi University and Thapar University students need.

We chose to focus on two universities on purpose. Rather than spreading thin across dozens of institutions with inconsistent data, EasySeek stays deep and accurate for the campuses it serves.
